Generated by `backend/scripts/train_fraud_model.py`. Re-run it after regenerating data.

## Data

- 10,000 synthetic Indian GST invoices from `scripts/generate_training_invoices.py` (938 fraudulent, 9.4%), 306 vendors.
- **Synthetic, not real invoices.** Vendor behaviour and fraud patterns are modelled on Indian B2B invoicing; real labelled invoices (approved/rejected by your reviewers) should replace or be added to this set before relying on the numbers below.
- Time-based split: first 8,000 uploads for training, last 2,000 (191 fraudulent) held out for testing.
